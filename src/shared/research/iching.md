# I Ching Research — Full Spectrum

## Overview
- **Name**: 易經 (Yì Jīng), "Book of Changes"
- **Origin**: Zhou dynasty China, ~1000-750 BCE (text), earlier divination roots
- **Core**: 64 hexagrams (六爻 guà) — 6-line figures of yin (broken) and yang (solid)
- **Trigrams**: 8 basic 3-line figures (八卦 bāguà), stacked in pairs to form hexagrams
- **Attributed to**: King Wen (hexagram order/names), Duke of Zhou (line texts), Confucius (Ten Wings commentary)

## Casting Methods

### Three-Coin Method (most popular)
- 3 identical coins, heads=3 (yang), tails=2 (yin)
- Toss 6 times, build hexagram from bottom to top
- Sum values each toss:
  - 6 (2+2+2) = Old Yin (changing) → broken line marked X
  - 7 (2+2+3 or 2+3+2 or 3+2+2) = Young Yang (stable) → solid line
  - 8 (3+3+2 or 3+2+3 or 2+3+3) = Young Yin (stable) → broken line
  - 9 (3+3+3) = Old Yang (changing) → solid line marked O
- Probability: 6=12.5%, 7=37.5%, 8=37.5%, 9=12.5% (equal change/stable per yin/yang)

### Yarrow Stalk Method (traditional/meditative)
- 50 stalks, 1 set aside (witness), 49 used
- 3 manipulations per line, 6 lines = 18 manipulations
- Probabilities differ from coin: 6=1/16, 7=5/16, 8=7/16, 9=3/16
- Favors static yin (most likely outcome)

### Other Methods
- **Single coin**: 6 tosses, heads=yang, tails=yin. No changing lines. Simpler.
- **Plum Blossom (Meihua Yishu)**: Derive hexagram from observed numbers/time/direction
- **Six Lines (Liu Yao/Na Jia)**: After hexagram, map lines to 12 earthly branches + 5 elements

## Reading Structure

1. **Primary Hexagram**: The initial hexagram from the 6 tosses
2. **Changing Lines**: Any 6 (old yin) or 9 (old yang) lines — these transform
3. **Secondary Hexagram**: Formed by changing all 6/9 lines to their opposites
4. **Nuclear Hexagram**: Inner meaning — formed by lines 2-4 (lower nuclear trigram) and 3-5 (upper nuclear trigram)

### Reading Order
1. Read the Judgment (卦辭) of Primary Hexagram — overall situation
2. Read the Image (象) — how to approach the situation
3. Read each changing line text — specific advice per changing line
4. Read the Judgment of Secondary Hexagram — direction of change
5. Synthesize primary + changing + secondary
