# Elder Futhark Runes — Full Spectrum Research

## Overview
- **Name**: Elder Futhark (named after first 6 runes: Fehu, Uruz, Thurisaz, Ansuz, Raidho, Kenaz)
- **Period**: 150–800 CE (Migration Period), Germanic peoples
- **Runes**: 24 characters, angular geometry (carved, no horizontal strokes — wood grain splitting)
- **Unicode block**: U+16A0–U+16FF (89 characters covering all runic systems)

## Three Ættir (groups of 8)

### Freyr's Aett (1-8) — Fehu to Wunjo
Themes: wealth, strength, communication, movement, craft, exchange, joy

### Heimdall's Aett (9-16) — Hagalaz to Sowilo
Themes: disruption, necessity, stillness, harvest, yew, fate, protection, sun

### Tyr's Aett (17-24) — Tiwaz to Othala
Themes: justice, birth, partnership, humanity, water, fertility, daybreak, inheritance

## Casting Methods

### Odin's Rune (Single draw)
Quick guidance, daily check-in, binary question insight. A single focal point.

### Three Norns Spread
- **Urd** (Past/root cause) — what created this terrain
- **Verdandi** (Present/agency) — the weaving process, active moment
- **Skuld** (Probable outcome) — consequence if present unchanged

### Five-Rune Cross
- Left: What you bring (resources/orientation)
- Above: Aspiration (what you're called toward)
- Right: Challenge (opposition)
- Below: Foundation (built on)
- Center: Heart of the matter

### Nine Worlds Layout (Yggdrasil)
Maps runes onto Norse cosmology:
- **Asgard** — Spiritual purpose, self, divinity
- **Alfheim** — Inspiration, creativity, light
- **Vanaheim** — Nature, relationships, flow
- **Midgard** — Physical reality, daily life, ego
- **Jotunheim** — External forces, chaos, challenges
- **Muspelheim** — Passion, action, drive, conflict
- **Niflheim** — Rest, stagnation, unconscious blocks
- **Svartalfheim** — Subconscious, resources, formation
- **Helheim** — Ancestors, deep hidden issues, death

### Free Cast (Scatter)
Throw all runes onto cloth, read those face-up. Clusters = related. Distance = separation. Face-down (merkstave) = hidden/blocked.

## Key Concepts
- **Merkstave**: Rune inverted (upside-down) — blocked, shadow, internalized meaning
- **Reversed**: Rune reversed on horizontal axis — different but not all traditions reverse
- **Aett**: Each group of 8 has a presiding deity (Freyr, Heimdall, Tyr)
- **Bind runes**: Two or more runes fused for combined meaning

## Visual Style Notes
- **Colors**: Blood red (#8b0000), ice blue (#4a90d9), amber gold (#c8a45c), runestone grey (#4a4a4a), deep black (#0a0a0a)
- **Textures**: Stone, wood grain, leather, weathered metal
- **Rune style**: Angular, carved appearance. Unicode runes render natively in modern browsers
- **Aesthetic**: Norse — think runestones, fjords, longhouses, ravens, Yggdrasil
